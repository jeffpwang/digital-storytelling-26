How Far Does a Snail Go?

Defining dispersal range
Fig. 1 2

First, dispersal.

Dispersal is the journey an animal makes away from the place it was born — out to wherever it will finally live, feed, and raise its own.

Every creature does it. Few do it as slowly as a snail.

Fig. 2 3

Now, its range.

An individual's dispersal range is the distance it covers from that birthplace across its whole life — the radius of its small, personal map.

Draw a circle around where it started. That circle is its world.

Fig. 3 4

And a snail's circle is small.
